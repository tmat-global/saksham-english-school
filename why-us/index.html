Empower Your Child's Future at Saksham English School.

Where Excellence Meets Quality Education in Renapur

1

Quality State Board Education

Saksham English School follows the Maharashtra State Board curriculum with English as the medium of instruction, offering Classes 1 through 9 with a focus on strong academic foundations.

2

Excellent Teacher-Student Ratio

With 20 experienced teachers for approximately 259 students, we maintain an outstanding 1:13 teacher-student ratio ensuring every child receives personalised attention and care.

3

Modern Facilities Available

Safe and well-equipped classrooms, computer labs, science lab, library, maths lab, and smart classrooms to make the school experience enriching and stress-free for every student.

4

Holistic Development with Strong Values

We blend quality education with Indian cultural values, preparing students for success at every stage — local or beyond. Co-curricular activities, sports, and cultural programs ensure all-round growth.
