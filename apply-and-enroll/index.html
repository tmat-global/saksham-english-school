Admissions Policy 2025-26

Admission to Saksham English School is open to students who demonstrate the ability to access and benefit from the quality education and programs offered by the school.

Criteria for Admission

  1. Availability of seats in appropriate classes and programs
  2. Meeting all admission procedures and qualifications
  3. Meeting all financial obligations
  4. Saksham English School will accept students from Early Years
  5. Parents will be invited for an interview with the Admissions Committee
  6. Final decision to be made by Principal

Documents Required for Admission

  1. The Application Form should be completed, signed, and submitted along with the following documentation
  2. 2 passport size photographs
  3. Copy of Birth Certificate
  4. Previous School Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate
  5. For Foreigners: Copy of Passport with valid student visa
  6. For Indians: Copy of the Aadhaar Card
  7. Copy of the final performance report (previous 2 years if applicable)
  8. Parents identification, which matches the information provided on the Application Form
