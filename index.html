Vision & Mission

Saksham English School is devoted to providing holistic education and empowering students to be responsible citizens. We believe in an inclusive curriculum and a hands-on approach to learning. Our aim is to prepare students for the future through a balanced combination of theoretical and practical education. We help students become disciplined citizens who think right, stay focused and have a deep abiding faith because we believe each child is uniquely creative.

Objective

One of the most important objectives of education is to create agents of change who will improve the fabric of society. Saksham English School is committed to moulding students as lifelong learners who will carry the flame of knowledge all their lives, igniting the minds of people around them. Most importantly, we inculcate an understanding of gender equality and women empowerment in all our students so they learn to respect everybody from their early years.

Our Motto

Our school's motto is Dignity and Wisdom. These virtues are essential to imbibe holistic development in our students so they can become responsible citizens. We believe that dignity stands for high rank and position, while wisdom stands for knowledge, experience and the power of applying them in everyday life for the betterment of society.

About Us

Saksham English School was established in the year 2019 and is located in Rupchand Nagar, Renapur, Latur, Maharashtra, beside the Renapur Bus Stand. The school is a private, co-educational institution recognised under Private Unaided management and follows the Maharashtra State Board curriculum.

Saksham English School provides education from Class 1 to Class 9 with English as the medium of instruction. With 14 well-equipped classrooms, approximately 259 students, and 20 dedicated teachers, the school maintains an excellent teacher-student ratio of 1:13, ensuring every child receives personalised attention and care.

Our school is committed to shaping bright futures through quality education, strong values, and a nurturing and supportive learning environment for every student in our care.
