Principal's Message

Education is not only to create an environment which helps students learn but also inspires them to recognise and explore their own potential.

Our school is a symbol of progressive and quality education. We aim at overall development of students, i.e. development in all the five domains — Physical, Mental, Emotional, Social and Moral. We strive to provide a learning conducive environment which enables children to self enquire and explore their latent talent and bloom to their fullest potential.

Believing in harmonious growth of children we put equal emphasis on academics, sports, life skills and values. We impart technologically embedded education and constantly work on pedagogical practices. Children get ample exposure to smart classroom teaching besides conventional classroom teaching.

In addition to academics, sports and other co-curricular activities are regularly organised. We motivate all children to participate in a plethora of activities ranging from literary activities, dance, drama, variety of sports events. Our school is scaling new heights with excellent professionals training our students in co-curricular activities like Football, Basketball, Cricket and many more indoor and outdoor games.

With an objective to boost mass participation and provide an opportunity to each and every child, thematic class assemblies are organised for all classes spread throughout the session. The school has also formed various clubs to strengthen child's academics and other performances.

Most importantly at school we are a team of dedicated teachers and staff committed to the welfare of students in our care. For us teaching is a passion and a calling. The importance of instilling good values in our students is our prime objective. We want our students to have fond memories of their time spent with us.
