Campus Life at SES

Computer Lab

We have computer labs with systems with internet connection exclusively for students. Students visit the computer lab during a designated class. In addition, SES has a resource lab for High School students. Dedicated and trained teachers are always at hand to help students with research and project work. SES is also WiFi enabled.

Computer Lab

Smart Classroom

The Smart Board interactive whiteboard operates as part of a system that includes the interactive whiteboard, a computer, a projector, and whiteboarding software. Experience an immersive and technologically advanced learning environment that engages students and enhances educational experiences.

Smart Classroom

Extra Curricular Activities (ECA)

Extra Curricular Activities is scheduled to fulfill, nurture and complete a holistic co-curricular approach for the skill development of every child at Saksham English School. With a wide choice of performing arts, students are given the opportunity to journey their field of interest with talented outsourced trainers.

Activities

Science Lab

Our school provides opportunities to our students to learn the various methods and processes. We combine classroom or textbook teaching with science laboratory experiments to derive the best results for our students. Science lab experiments and science laboratory equipment aid in developing scientific learning amongst students, and in cultivating deeper and profound interest in the field.

Science Lab

Library

SES is the first to host an e-library. The library is a books paradise with novels, magazines, encyclopedias and subject-specific books. The friendly librarians are ever willing to offer a helping hand to students. Explore a wealth of knowledge at your fingertips, with our innovative e-library providing easy access to a vast array of resources.

Library

Maths Lab

Maths lab is an area where students learn and enjoy mathematics through shapes, patterns, board games etc. We conduct Maths training workshops on the latest topics and trends to the school maths faculty. We can also customise the existing Maths lab according to the requirement of our students.

Maths Lab
